# Departs iOS roadmap

## Product direction

On 16 November 2026 the owner defined Departs as two screens showing which transport is available around you. Each screen has a square map above a short list. Home shows nearby lines leaving within an hour, once per destination at the nearest qualifying stop. A row opens the line's walking map and next four departures. The [approved plan](plans/ios-two-screens.md) specifies the exact selection rules, states and acceptance gates.

Later that day the owner made it stricter: "This app is minimalistic and opinionated. It looks in a 400m radius, period." The wider search and place search are gone. With location off, the app shows pinned routes only.

The owner retired the browser client on 15 November. iOS is the sole product client. Spacefast serves it at `api.departs.now`. The owner retired the legacy Vercel host without forwarding. Historical web designs stay in `docs/archive/web/`.

## Current implementation

- Home selects the eight rows in the Vienna sample from 17 platforms. A single batch replaces departure fan-out; opening takes one stops request, one batch and one on-device MapKit walking request per listed physical stop. The three backend entries serve the root notice, nearby stops and departures. HERE supplies the transit data.
- Fresh rows show a single countdown. Saved rows retain their position and show clock times with a recovery action. Cached content appears before networking.
- The line screen keeps the full destination visible, shows up to four departures and opens Apple Maps walking directions to the platform. Live times carry a symbol and full-minute delay. There is no scheduled label or leave advice.
- Pins in the 400 m area have rows on home. "Pinned elsewhere" shows the other pins within 50 km of the area centre, or all pins if there is no centre. App Shortcuts reach pins from every city. Pins are still a product trial under O8. See the [sixth amendment](plans/ios-two-screens.md#sixth-amendment-pins-in-the-current-city) for O24 and the choices made while building it. [PR #42](https://github.com/p3ob7o/Departs/pull/42) is merged, and **Departs 1.2 (13) followed on 22 November: uploaded, VALID and IN_BETA_TESTING for internal testers.** The [release record](releases/1.2-13.md) lists its checks and the upload. There is no search of any kind. Since the owner's corrections of 17 and 18 November, home has no top bar. On iPhone the square map starts under the status bar and stays centred on you. On a phone with a Dynamic Island or a notch the map continues above the square to the top of the screen, blurred under the status bar by iOS (O18). Since 19 November no ring marks the radius (O23), and the line screen has the same frame, with its back, line, walk and pin under the map (O22). Since 27 November the line map is locked in every layout (O28).
- Departs runs in one resizable iPad window in all orientations. Supporting several windows requires another owner decision. Both screens stack in tall or narrow windows and split evenly in wider landscape windows. In a split the map fills the left side and the controls and list share the right. In a short stacked window the line map shrinks to keep two departures visible below a long destination. At accessibility text sizes a stacked map scrolls away and a split map stays beside the list. iPhone remains portrait only. See the [seventh amendment](plans/ios-two-screens.md#seventh-amendment-ipad-windows).

See [native behavior](ios-fast-answers.md) and the [final validation](plans/ios-two-screens-phase-3-validation.md). The old fast-answer roadmap's advice, optional-map and split-view work is superseded.

## Release sequence

The test baseline was Departs 1.1 (6). After the implementation checks passed, the owner requested the update on 16 November. **Departs 1.2 (7) is uploaded, VALID and IN_BETA_TESTING for internal testers.** The [release record](releases/1.2-7.md) contains package, production and processing verification.

Plan PR #26 and additive backend PR #27 merged into `main`, and Vienna/Berlin production checks passed before upload. Native home #28, line #29, cleanup #30 and release #31 form the implementation stack. The owner explicitly approved landing all four in `main` in that order, retargeting after each previous merge. Merging into an already-merged phase branch does not update `main`.

The uploaded build uses the tested release branch. Backend-first is satisfied. Complete the remaining physical-device checks with TestFlight; no external beta review or public App Store release was submitted.

**Departs 1.2 (8) followed on 17 November: uploaded, VALID and IN_BETA_TESTING for internal testers.** It removes place search and the wider search, and fixes two defects found in the test of 1.2 (7): the list started over about every five minutes on a phone, and a coarse location fix left home waiting forever. [PR #32](https://github.com/p3ob7o/Departs/pull/32) is merged. The [release record](releases/1.2-8.md) lists the checks and seven open findings that need the owner's decision.

**Four corrections followed the owner's use of 1.2 (8) on 17 November**, recorded as O13 to O16 in the [plan's second amendment](plans/ios-two-screens.md#second-amendment-the-map-at-the-top): home loses its bar and the map is centred on you at all times, a stop's name leaves with its last line, destinations start at one x, and times read "7 min". O17 followed on 18 November: badges show only the line's identifier, so "FlixBus N70" reads "N70". [PR #34](https://github.com/p3ob7o/Departs/pull/34) is merged, and **Departs 1.2 (9) followed on 18 November: uploaded, VALID and IN_BETA_TESTING for internal testers.** The [release record](releases/1.2-9.md) lists its checks and the upload.

**The map under the status bar followed on 18 November**, recorded as O18 in the [plan's third amendment](plans/ios-two-screens.md#third-amendment-the-map-under-the-status-bar). The owner saw four treatments captured from the app and chose the blur iOS draws by itself. [PR #36](https://github.com/p3ob7o/Departs/pull/36) is merged, and **Departs 1.2 (10) followed on 19 November: uploaded, VALID and IN_BETA_TESTING for internal testers.** The [release record](releases/1.2-10.md) lists its checks and the upload.

**A tap on a map label followed on 19 November**, recorded as O19 in the [plan's fourth amendment](plans/ios-two-screens.md#fourth-amendment-a-tap-on-a-map-label). With 1.2 (10) on his phone, the owner found that taps on some labels did nothing. Each badge now opens its line, or offers its directions when the line runs more than one way, and a label that is already selected answers the next tap. [PR #38](https://github.com/p3ob7o/Departs/pull/38) is merged, and **Departs 1.2 (11) followed the same day: uploaded, VALID and IN_BETA_TESTING for internal testers.** The [release record](releases/1.2-11.md) lists its checks and the upload.

**The line screen's controls moved under its map later on 19 November**, recorded as O20 to O23 in the [plan's fifth amendment](plans/ios-two-screens.md#fifth-amendment-the-line-screens-controls-under-its-map). With 1.2 (11) on his phone, the owner asked for right-aligned countdowns, a choice between Google Maps and Apple Maps that is remembered, the line screen's controls under a map framed as on home, and no ring on home. [PR #40](https://github.com/p3ob7o/Departs/pull/40) is merged, and **Departs 1.2 (12) followed the same evening: uploaded, VALID and IN_BETA_TESTING for internal testers.** The [release record](releases/1.2-12.md) lists its checks and the upload.

**Destinations with a plus sign.** A line to a destination such as "S+U Pankow" showed no departures, because the app sent the plus unencoded and the backend read it as a space. [PR #51](https://github.com/p3ob7o/Departs/pull/51) changes both sides. Its backend fix reached production on 24 November 2026 and repairs installed builds: when it filters a line, a plus and a space count as the same character. The app's own encoding of a plus as `%2B` ships with 1.3 (14).

**Departs 1.3 (14) followed on 24 November: uploaded, VALID and IN_BETA_TESTING for internal testers.** The owner chose version 1.3 for the first build with iPad support. It is the first build named Departs Now ([PR #50](https://github.com/p3ob7o/Departs/pull/50)), adds one resizable iPad window with stacked and side-by-side layouts ([PR #48](https://github.com/p3ob7o/Departs/pull/48)) and the plus-sign encoding ([PR #51](https://github.com/p3ob7o/Departs/pull/51)). The [release record](releases/1.3-14.md) lists its checks and what remains unverified on iPad. The record also holds the upload. The icon still shows the word "Departs": the owner chose to ship with it.

**Departs 1.3 (15) followed on 26 November: uploaded, VALID and IN_BETA_TESTING for internal testers.** It is the first build that calls `api.departs.now` on Spacefast ([PR #55](https://github.com/p3ob7o/Departs/pull/55)). It retries a rate-limit response once and keeps stops and walks available through local caching. Builds through 1.3 (14) used the now-retired Vercel host. The [release record](releases/1.3-15.md) lists the simulator checks, the live check on the new hostname in Vienna and Berlin, the upload, and what remains unverified on a physical device.

**Departs 1.3 (16) followed on 27 November: uploaded, VALID and IN_BETA_TESTING for internal testers.** It is the first build that gets walking routes from MapKit on the device ([PR #63](https://github.com/p3ob7o/Departs/pull/63)); builds through 1.3 (15) asked the backend, which called Mapbox. Departures no longer wait for a walking route, and no route is requested while location access is denied or restricted. The [release record](releases/1.3-16.md) lists the checks, the upload, and what remains unverified on a physical device. The owner confirmed build 16 works on his phone and accepted the loss of walking routes in build 1.3 (15) when the backend endpoint is removed.

**Departs 1.3 (17) followed the same day: uploaded, VALID and IN_BETA_TESTING for internal testers.** It locks the line screen's map, as the owner asked after using 1.3 (16), and fixes a crash when a saved platform is opened from very far away ([PR #67](https://github.com/p3ob7o/Departs/pull/67)). The [release record](releases/1.3-17.md) lists the checks, the upload, and what remains unverified on a physical device. The backend's walking-directions endpoint and Mapbox were removed the same night ([PR #66](https://github.com/p3ob7o/Departs/pull/66)).

## Next decisions and checks

- Review A34 to A42 in the [fifth amendment](plans/ios-two-screens.md#fifth-amendment-the-line-screens-controls-under-its-map): which times share the countdown column, when the map-app choice is offered and how it is remembered, the plain controls under the line map, and the line screen at accessibility sizes. On a phone, check the long press on the walk button, Google Maps' walking directions, and the swipe back from the left edge.
- Review A28 to A33 in the [fourth amendment](plans/ios-two-screens.md#fourth-amendment-a-tap-on-a-map-label): what "+N" offers, what VoiceOver gets, when a label is ringed, and which of two overlapping labels takes a tap.
- Review A27 in the [third amendment](plans/ios-two-screens.md#third-amendment-the-map-under-the-status-bar): an iPhone SE keeps the square alone, because its status bar is too short for the blur. On a phone, check that the clock and the status icons stay readable over the map in light and in dark.
- Rewrite the [iPhone Duo plan, PR #18](https://github.com/p3ob7o/Departs/pull/18) for map and list side by side. The older iPad proposal predates the seventh amendment.
- Decide the iPad follow-ups to O27: whether Departs should support several windows, and whether the iPhone Duo, which gets the same one-window setting, should keep it.
- Review A20, A23 and A24 in the [second amendment](plans/ios-two-screens.md#second-amendment-the-map-at-the-top): what the map centres on, how a pinned stop name looks, and which words of a line's name are kept.
- Decide the seven open findings in the [1.2 (8) record](releases/1.2-8.md#open-findings-not-changed-in-this-build). The first is a defect: a stop with no departures is shown as a failed load, seen at Berlin Alexanderplatz.
- With build 8 on a phone: leave the app open in one place for ten minutes, walk a few hundred metres, and try Location off and Precise Location off. With the next build, check that the map follows a walk smoothly and stays put with Precise Location off.
- Check taps on map labels and their choice of directions, long destinations and empty nighttime results on a phone. Observe whether people find the available service without opening several lines.
- Verify VoiceOver speech, haptics, GPS accuracy, foreground return, Shortcuts discovery and Home Screen icon appearances on a signed device.
- Measure cold/warm startup on mobile data and provider quota/cost under the batch model. Check a capped hub and operators with realtime coverage. The Vienna sample supplied scheduled data only.
- Decide whether to keep pins after observing use. Widgets, Live Activities, notifications, accounts, German and journey planning remain out of scope.

No analytics SDK or tracking service is added.
