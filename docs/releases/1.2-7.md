## What changes for testers

Home now shows a square map above nearby lines leaving within an hour. A service appears once at the nearest qualifying stop. Opening a line shows its walking map and next four departures. Saved times remain readable after a failed refresh; pins and search remain available. Leave advice and idle-line disclosures are removed. iPhone returns to portrait only.

Requires iOS 26. The app remains iPhone-only and English-only. No Duo/iPad adaptation, Mac/Vision availability, external beta review or public App Store release is included.
