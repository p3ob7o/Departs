# Departs 1.3 (17) TestFlight

Departs Now 1.3 (17) locks the map on the line screen. The owner asked for it after using 1.3 (16): the map for a single stop could be dragged with a finger, and it should be locked like the map on the first screen. The marketing version stays 1.3 and the build number advances to 17.

## What changes since 1.3 (16)

All of it comes from [PR #67](https://github.com/p3ob7o/Departs/pull/67).

- The line screen's map takes no pan, zoom, rotation or tilt, on iPhone and in both iPad layouts. This replaces the earlier choice to keep that map interactive (O28 in the [plan](../plans/ios-two-screens.md)).
- The map keeps the platform, the walking start and the whole route line in view by itself. It refits when any of them changes or when the map changes size.
- A crash is fixed. With the rider and a saved platform on opposite sides of the Pacific, or with a short walk across the antimeridian, the map asked MapKit for an impossible region and the app closed. Builds through 1.3 (16) have this fault. The map now measures the short way around and bounds its region.
- At 5 km or more from the platform, the map shows the platform alone at its usual scale. Earlier builds zoomed out until both were in view. The app never requested a walking route at that distance.
- At accessibility text sizes a drag that starts on the map scrolls the list. The swipe back from the left edge still works over the map.

The backend is unchanged by this build.

## What to test

Open a line and try to drag or pinch its map: it should not move. Check that the stop, your position and the whole route line are in view, also after you walk a little and after rotating an iPad. Swipe back from the left edge starting over the map.

## Verification before the upload

- **Same code as tested.** The app source tree of `main` at the merge of PR #67 (`55a78be`) equals that of the PR head `a45de0c`. This release changes the build number and adds this record, nothing else.
- **Native suite on the PR head, run by the releasing session.** iPhone 17 Pro, iOS 27.0, on 27 November 2026, on a new simulator with Maps granted location access: 166 passed (122 unit, 44 UI), 0 failed, 4 iPad-only tests skipped. The gesture test, both crash regression tests and the Apple Maps handoff test passed.
- **iPad, by the independent review.** iPad Air 13-inch (M4), iOS 27.0: 9 passed, 0 failed, 0 skipped, covering the four tests skipped on iPhone.
- **Lock, by the independent review on the first PR head `2cc8acc`.** 60 gesture checks across iPhone portrait, iPad stacked and iPad side by side, each at standard and accessibility text sizes: drag, pinch, double tap, double-tap-and-drag, two-finger tap, rotation, tilt, long press, and drags starting on a pin or on the route. Nothing moved the map. The gesture test fails on the code before the change: a drag moved the platform pin 70.67 points.
- **Region calculation, by the independent review.** 22,500 generated sets of valid positions all gave a region that MapKit accepted without an exception.
- **Ordinary walks unchanged.** On iPad the visible area for 20 m, 350 m and 4,999 m walks matched the first PR head to floating-point precision.
- **Release simulator build** of the PR head: zero warnings, the same as `main` before the change. The Release binary holds no test hook.

Not run for this build: the suite after the build-number change, and the iPhone SE simulator.

## Unverified

- The build on a physical iPhone or iPad. Every check above ran on a simulator.
- Near the poles, or with a route that bends to the antimeridian and back, the platform can end up outside the visible map. Earlier builds have the same fault. The review could not produce it with ordinary rider data.
- A position that wavers around 5 km from the platform makes the map switch between its two scales on each refresh.
- One run of the unit suite on the author's machine aborted once in a walking-route test. It did not recur in 30 isolated and 12 full repetitions, and the crash report does not point at the app.
