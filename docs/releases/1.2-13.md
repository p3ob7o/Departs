# Departs 1.2 (13) TestFlight

On 22 November 2026, the owner asked for home to show pinned stops for the city the rider is in. The request is O24 in the plan's [sixth amendment](../plans/ios-two-screens.md#sixth-amendment-pins-in-the-current-city). Later that day he was shown A43, A44, A45 and A47 as built and answered "ship". He has not reviewed A46 or A48 to A51.

The build number goes from 12 to 13. The marketing version stays 1.2. The backend is unchanged. The owner approved [PR #42](https://github.com/p3ob7o/Departs/pull/42) and the upload with the word "ship" at 21:56 CET on 22 November.

## What changes for testers

- **Home shows pins for the current city.** Pins at stops in the 400 m area still have their own rows. “Pinned elsewhere” lists and counts the remaining pins up to 50 km from the area centre. It updates when the area changes, and an open sheet closes if its list empties. With no area centre, the sheet lists all saved pins.
- **Saved pins keep working.** Shortcuts and existing links can open pins in any city. The filter uses the stop coordinates already saved with each pin.
