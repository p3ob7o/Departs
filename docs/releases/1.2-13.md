# Departs 1.2 (13) TestFlight

On 22 November 2026, the owner asked for home to show pinned stops for the city the rider is in. The request is O24 in the plan's [sixth amendment](../plans/ios-two-screens.md#sixth-amendment-pins-in-the-current-city). Later that day he was shown A43, A44, A45 and A47 as built and answered "ship". He has not reviewed A46 or A48 to A51.

The build number goes from 12 to 13. The marketing version stays 1.2. The backend is unchanged. The owner approved [PR #42](https://github.com/p3ob7o/Departs/pull/42) and the upload with the word "ship" at 21:56 CET on 22 November.

## What changes for testers

- **Home shows pins for the current city.** Pins at stops in the 400 m area still have their own rows. “Pinned elsewhere” lists and counts the remaining pins up to 50 km from the area centre. It updates when the area changes, and an open sheet closes if its list empties. With no area centre, the sheet lists all saved pins.
- **Saved pins keep working.** Shortcuts and existing links can open pins in any city. The filter uses the stop coordinates already saved with each pin.

## iOS 26.2 after the upload

On 22 November the owner asked for build 13 to be tested on iOS 26 and for the Apple Maps hand-off to be tested. The iOS 26.2 simulator runtime (23C52) was installed again from the copy downloaded earlier that day. Agents ran the suites with Xcode 27.2 (27B52) on `f87f0a2`, which has build 13's app code, on simulators created for each run. Times are CET.

| Simulator | iOS | Unit passed | UI passed | UI failed | Finished |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| iPhone 17 Pro | 26.2 | 84 | 39 | 1 | 22 November, 23:08 |
| iPhone SE (3rd generation) | 26.2 | 84 | 40 | 0 | 22 November, 23:21 |
| iPhone 17 Pro | 26.2 | 84 | 40 | 0 | 23 November, 00:49 |
| iPhone 17 Pro | 26.2 | 84 | 40 | 0 | 23 November, 01:32 |

No tests were skipped. `testWalkingDirectionsOpensAppleMaps` passed in all four runs. The German stress test passed on the SE at 23:22 on 22 November.

**The one failure** was `testHomeHasNoBarAndOffersNoWiderSearch` in the first run. Home showed "Couldn't update nearby stops" and "Try again". The app's log shows that its request to the suite's local fixture server was reset three times within half a second, about a second after location was allowed, and ended with "The network connection was lost". Every other UI test passed in that run. The test passed three times of three when rerun alone on the same simulator.

A second agent tried to reproduce the failure on new iPhone 17 Pro simulators with iOS 26.2. It did not come back in 62 runs:

- the full suite twice, the second time with nothing else running on the Mac (the last two rows above);
- the test alone, 20 of 20;
- the test right after `testFirstStopsFailureShowsErrorAndTryAgain`, as in the suite, 20 of 20 pairs;
- the test alone while 33 clean builds of the app ran beside it, 20 of 20.

Why the fixture server reset those connections is not established. During the first run another agent was running UI tests on other simulators of this Mac. The fixture server does not log the requests it receives, so the suite's own log could not show what it did with them.

**The Apple Maps hand-off.** A third agent tested the hand-off itself on new simulators of five types: iPhone 17 Pro with iOS 26.2, 27.0 and 27.2, and iPhone SE with iOS 26.2 and 27.0. On each one Apple Maps showed a walking route to Siebensterngasse. A reviewer's run showed the same on an iPhone SE with iOS 27.2. The failures recorded above came from the test, for three reasons:

- On a new simulator Maps can show its first-run sheet 16 seconds after the hand-off, after the test has stopped looking for sheets.
- On the iPhone SE with iOS 27.0 Maps shows a compact route screen that the test did not recognize.
- On iOS 27.2 a Contacts prompt for Maps has two "Don't Allow" buttons. The suite's launch step taps that label, finds two buttons and fails. This is what stopped the three retries on iOS 27.2 above.

[PR #44](https://github.com/p3ob7o/Departs/pull/44) reworks the test. Its review on 23 November found that the first version still failed on an iPhone SE with iOS 27.2 and that the launch step was unchanged. The second version fixed both. It passed as the first test on new simulators of all six types and in full suites on four of them, and it failed as required when the hand-off was broken on purpose with driving mode and with a wrong destination name. The owner approved it, and it was merged at 18:18 CET on 23 November.

The test now waits up to 120 seconds for Maps' first sheet on a new simulator. In 37 of 43 recorded launches the sheet came within 19 seconds. Twice it took about 90 seconds, both times while another project's builds were running on the Mac.

Not tested on iOS 26: a phone, and the pin change on live data.

On 23 November the owner decided that later builds are not tested on iOS 26 before release. iOS 26 remains the minimum OS.
