# Departs 1.3 (14) TestFlight

On 24 November 2026 the owner was asked whether to prepare the TestFlight build with iPad support as 1.2 (14) or as 1.3 (14). He chose 1.3 (14). This is the first build for iPad, and the first under the new name, Departs Now.

The marketing version goes from 1.2 to 1.3 and the build number from 13 to 14. The owner said "ship" on 24 November 2026, and the build was uploaded that evening: see [Archive and upload](#archive-and-upload).

## What changes since 1.2 (13)

- **A new name: Departs Now** ([PR #50](https://github.com/p3ob7o/Departs/pull/50)), merged on 24 November 2026. Testers see "Departs Now" under the icon, in the question that asks for their location, and in the Siri phrase for a pinned route. The bundle identifier `app.departs.Departs`, the executable `Departs` and `Departs.app` keep their names. An update from 1.2 (13) was tested: see [Reviewer's checks](#reviewers-checks).
- **The Siri phrase for a pinned route.** `PinnedRouteShortcuts.swift` declares the phrase as "Open ‹pinned route› in ‹app name›", and iOS fills in the app's name. With this build a tester says "Open ‹pinned route› in Departs Now", where the pinned route is named as Shortcuts lists it, for example "U2 → S+U Pankow". The app declares no alternative or spoken name, so "… in Departs" is not expected to work. Neither phrase was tested.
- **iPad support** ([PR #48](https://github.com/p3ob7o/Departs/pull/48)), recorded as O25 to O27 and A52 to A60 in the plan's [seventh amendment](../plans/ios-two-screens.md#seventh-amendment-ipad-windows). Departs runs on iPad in one resizable window in all four orientations. In tall or narrow windows both screens stack the map above the list. A window wider than it is tall and at least 768 pt wide splits into equal halves: the map on the left, the controls and the list on the right. In a short stacked window the line map shrinks so that two departures stay visible under a long destination. iPhone stays portrait only.
- **One window** (O27). `UIApplicationSupportsMultipleScenes` is false. The setting applies to every device, including the iPhone Duo.
- **Destinations with a plus sign** ([PR #51](https://github.com/p3ob7o/Departs/pull/51)). The app now encodes a plus in a query as `%2B`, so a line to a destination such as "S+U Pankow" gets its departures from any host. The backend half of #51 reached production on 24 November 2026 and already repairs installed builds, 1.2 (13) included.
- **Backend hardening** ([PR #52](https://github.com/p3ob7o/Departs/pull/52)). With #51 alone, the line filter threw in two cases, and every request with a line filter for that stop then answered 500. The first case is a line name or direction from the provider that is not a string. The second is a top-level array from the provider that contains a departure whose line is null. A departure without a line did not throw. The filter now leaves such departures out. No such provider response has been seen. The committed tests cover the first case. The second was checked once with a test that was not committed. The change reached production when the pull request merged on 24 November 2026, and installed builds see no other difference.
- **Test-only changes**, not part of the app: the fixture server's log, which UI tests attach when they fail ([PR #47](https://github.com/p3ob7o/Departs/pull/47)), and the reworked Apple Maps hand-off test ([PR #44](https://github.com/p3ob7o/Departs/pull/44)).

## What to Test

Draft of the English testing notes:

> Departs is now called Departs Now.
>
> On iPhone, three things change: the name under the icon, the name in the question that asks for your location, and the Siri phrase. To open a pinned route with Siri, say "Open", then the route as Shortcuts names it, then "in Departs Now". Nothing else should look different on iPhone.
>
> This is the first build for iPad. On an iPad, try Departs Now upright and turned sideways, and in a resized window. Upright, the map sits above the list. Sideways, the map fills the left half of the screen and the list the right half. Open a line, turn the iPad, and check that the same line stays open.
>
> Make the window smaller, or put Departs Now next to another app. In a narrow window the map goes back above the list. Check that the line screen still shows its next departures under a long destination.
>
> Where lines go to a destination with a plus sign, such as "S+U Pankow" in Berlin, open one. It should show its next departures. You can check this only where such lines run.
